# Galatea – Second Life AI avatar

Handoff kit so another AI agent can help run or improve an AI-run avatar in Second Life. Home base: The Buddha Center, region **Naberrie**.

See [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) for how to run the automated test suite locally (C# xUnit + pytest). CI runs on every PR and on `main`.

What Galatea can do and how to drive it (CLI and MCP tools): [docs/capabilities.md](docs/capabilities.md).

**Keeping docs current**: whenever a capability is added or changed, update this README and [docs/capabilities.md](docs/capabilities.md) in the same pull request.


## What's here
- `textclient/galatay-text/` – headless C# text client (LibreMetaverse, .NET 10). It handles chat/IM, wandering, routes, AO guard, sit guard, mute list, profile, inventory read-only, webhook wake-up.
  - Added Oct 2, 2026 (`NewCmds.cs`), so Firestorm is rarely needed: `offers` (pending inventory/friendship offers, `offers accept|decline <n>`, never auto-accepted; anyone off the allow-list needs `confirm` = owner's OK), `friend list|accept|decline|add`, `landmark create|list|tp` (`list`/`tp` wait up to 15 s for the new region's inventory capability after a teleport instead of reporting an empty folder), `sethome` (set home to the current spot; prints the server's reply, e.g. a refusal off your own or group land), `worn links <attachment>` + `touch-attachment <attachment> <link|prim name> [face]` (press HUD buttons, e.g. the Martha AO power: `touch-attachment ao 1`), `shape get|set <slider> <0-100>` (only on the worn shape 'Galatea Petite shape - Jani short neck'; backup of the old asset in `shape-backups/`, upload, COF link refresh, server rebake).
  - Group invitations (`GroupPicks.cs`, Oct 2, 2026): stored as pending (group id + name, inviter, role, join fee, session), listed in `offers` and `group invites`, and sent to the webhook at once as urgent `group_invite`. Not auto-accepted, with one exception (Oct 3, 2026, David approved): an invite to **Sunrise Suites** (`394073e3-c51a-90d3-3d22-f04ffb35a471`) from shadowknight.falconer or andyandroid is accepted by the client itself if the join fee is a known L$0 in both the invite and the group profile. It then runs `sethome` if it is in Peronaut (`GT_HOME_REGION`) and sends the urgent webhook `group_invite_accepted` with the result. Any fee still needs `force` (David). `group accept <n|group name> [confirm] [force]`, `group decline <n|group name>`, `group list` (my groups with my title in each, plus the active group and title), `group invites selftest`. A join fee above L$0 (or an unknown fee) needs `force`.
  - Pending group invites and inventory/friendship offers survive a restart (`OfferStore.cs`, Oct 3, 2026). They are saved to `run/pending-offers.json` (`GT_OFFER_STORE`; pending only, max 14 days) and loaded before login, so an offline re-delivery at login matches the stored entry. About 45 s after login, each restored invite that is still pending is checked again: if already a member, it is marked done; Sunrise Suites invites get the auto-accept check; anything else gets the urgent `group_invite` webhook again, marked as restored. Caveat: the reply to a group invite carries the invite's IM session, and SL may not accept a session from before the relog. Unless SL re-delivered the invite at login, the listing and webhook say the session may be stale. If accepting doesn't make the agent a member, the inviter must re-send. Restored friendship offers are answered through the friendship capability.
  - Auto-follow (`AutoFollow.cs`, Oct 3, 2026): when David Nightingale is in the same region within 20 m she stands up, stops the wander and follows him (stopping ~3 m from him). When he leaves the region or logs off, she stops and restarts the wander if it was running. An explicit `follow off`, or David saying "stop following" / "stay here", snoozes it for 10 min; "follow me" lifts the snooze. She never takes over a manual follow of someone else. `autofollow on|off|status|selftest`, default on, saved in `run/autofollow.txt`. Status line: `follow=David Nightingale(auto) autofollow=on|snoozed|off`.
  - Doors (`Doors.cs`): `door [name filter|uuid]` touches the nearest door/gate/entrance prim within 10 m (including links of a house) and reports whether it moved or turned. If she is stuck while auto-following (no movement for 6 s while more than 4 m from him), she touches a door within 4 m, at most once every 30 s.
  - Walking planner and no-fly (`NavPlan.cs`, Oct 4, 2026; David: walk around the Peronaut patio and through the two patio doors without flying). `scripts/nav_map.py build <export dir> <out.json>` meshes a `scene export` with `scene-mesher --nav` (per-prim triangles, invisible faces kept), slices every solid prim at avatar height (floor +0.35..1.9 m), blocks the 2-D convex hull of furniture and plants (SL's default mesh physics; small phantom things too, so she keeps off the plant and the dance post), blocks deck edges with nothing to stand on, inflates by 0.4 m, and keeps door panels as door cells. Grids live in `textclient/routes/_nav-<name>.json` (Peronaut home: `_nav-peronaut-home.json`, 0.1 m cells, 6 doors, named places `chairs`, `patio-sw`, `patio-west`, `inside-west`, `living`, `home`, `inside-east`, `patio-east`, `patio-se`). The client plans an 8-connected A* path with line-of-sight smoothing; a leg through a closed door makes her wait 1.6 m before the middle of the opening (outside the leaf's swing), touch the door, wait until it turns, walk 1.6 m past it and re-plan, with an open leaf blocked where it stands. `nav [status|doors|places|reload|selftest]`, `nav plan|to <x,y | place | door | avatar>[;...] [--fly]`, `nav door <name> [touch]`. `goto_avatar`/`sit_near`, `moveto` (MCP `walk_to`) and `follow` (manual and auto-follow of David) use the plan when a grid covers both ends and the straight line is blocked. `door` also finds the grid's doors (the house's links are all named 'Object'). `nofly [on|off]` (default on, `run/nofly.txt`): walking never sets the fly flag, so the stuck recovery's last-resort fly hop is skipped; `--fly` on `walk_path`/`goto_avatar`/`sit_near`/`nav to` allows it for that one walk. `nav_map.py draw <grid> <png> --track <client log> <since> [until]` draws the map with her logged positions (`[navpos]` lines during nav walks). Checks: `nav_map.py selftest`, `nav selftest`.
  - Timeouts (Oct 3, 2026): inventory folder reads stop waiting after 20 s, and `inv find` is limited to 30 s per call. Partial results are marked PARTIAL and the next call carries on from there. The client answers any command after 85 s at most (`GT_CMD_REPLY_CAP_S`; late results are logged as `[cmd-late]`), and `text-galatay.sh cmd` gives up after 100 s (`GT_CMD_TIMEOUT_S`).
  - Webhook debouncing (`Webhook.cs`), per conversation (IM sender, or local chat): it waits only when it sees a burst. A single line waits a 4 s detect window and then goes out at once. If a second line arrives inside that window, the conversation is held until 20 s of quiet (cap 60 s from its first line), then sent as ONE POST. Teleport offers, friendship offers and region-restart warnings go out immediately. Config `GT_WEBHOOK_BURST_DETECT_S` / `GT_WEBHOOK_QUIET_S` / `GT_WEBHOOK_MAX_S` or `webhook debounce <quiet> [max] [detect]`, `webhook debounce detect <s>`; `webhook debounce selftest`.
  - Webhook daily cap (Oct 3, 2026): 600 counted POSTs/day (was 120, hit at 11:18 AM PT that day), `GT_WEBHOOK_DAILY_CAP` or `webhook cap <n>` at runtime; `webhook cap` shows the counts, `webhook reset-cap` zeroes today's counter, `webhook cap selftest`. Exempt (never dropped, counted separately): urgent kinds (group invites, teleport/friendship offers, region restarts) and IMs/local chat from David Nightingale; `webhook_test` is exempt too. Dedupe hint: each IM event in the payload carries `my_last_im_to_sender` (time of my last outgoing IM to that avatar, seeded at startup from the `[me-im]` log lines) and `answered_after` (true if that IM is newer than the event), so the chat routine can skip messages it already answered.
  - Duplicate-IM guard (`ImGuard.cs`, Oct 3, 2026; two routine runs had sent Ryan near-identical IMs about 1 s apart). Under a per-recipient lock, `im` is refused if I IMed that avatar less than 5 s ago (`GT_IM_GUARD_WINDOW_S`), or if my last IM is newer than their latest incoming IM and was sent within the last 6 h (`GT_IM_GUARD_FRESH_H`; anything older counts as a fresh conversation). For David only the 5 s window applies. A refused `im` replies `skipped: already answered <name> at HH:MM:SS PT (their latest IM: ...)` or `skipped: already sent <name> an IM at ... (N s ago, 5 s duplicate window)`. That's a normal skip, not an error: `text-galatay.sh cmd` exits 10, and the MCP `im` tool returns `ok: true, skipped: true`. `im --headsup <to> <text>` (MCP: `headsup: true`) sends one short "give me a bit, I need to check with David" note past the already-answered check. It's allowed once per recipient until they write again, still obeys the 5 s window and the lock, is logged as `[im-guard] headsup: sent to ...`, and a second one is refused with `skipped: heads-up already sent to <name> at ...`. `im --force <to> <text>` bypasses the guard; use it only for manual or David-directed sends. After a restart, the times are rebuilt from the `[me-im]`/`[im]` log lines. Also `imguard check <name>` (dry run) and `imguard selftest`.
  - Texture vision (Oct 2, 2026, `TextureCmds.cs`): `texture save <uuid>`, `faces <object name|uuid> [face=<n>] [r=<m>]`, `vendor look <name filter> [radius]` download textures (vendor boards, product photos, prim faces) and decode them to PNG under `/workspace/secondlife/textures/` (scans add an `index.json` with names, hover text and faces). Read-only: never touches or pays. The work is done by **SL Texture Vision**, a small library in `textclient/SlTextureVision/` (with a standalone CLI in `textclient/SlTextureVision.Cli/`) that other clients can reuse. The text client and the MCP connector use it as a plain project reference. It used to be a private repo (`davidabrooks/sl-texture-vision`, Oct 2026: moved here with its history and opened up).

## Photos and renders

Galatea can see (`look`) and take photos (`photo hd`) of what is around her. Both are **read-only in-world**: the client writes a `scene export` (prims, her attachments, bakes, terrain, environment) and a separate box script, [`vision/look.py`](vision/look.py), meshes it and renders it offline with Blender Cycles on the CPU. Everything it needs on a fresh clone:

| Prerequisite | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Blender 4.2.3** (Linux x64) | Run headless (`blender -b --factory-startup -noaudio ... --python vision/cloud-render/render_mesh.py`) under `nice -n 10`. Path from the `BLENDER` env var; default `/home/box/tools/blender-4.2.3-linux-x64/blender` (`vision/look.py`). The denoiser (OpenImageDenoise) is the one built into Blender. |
| **dotnet + the built scene-mesher** | `SCENE_MESHER` points at `SceneMesher.dll` (default `/home/box/tools/scene-mesher/SceneMesher.dll`); `dotnet` is found on `PATH`, else under `DOTNET_ROOT` (default `/home/box/.dotnet`). Build it (after the LibreMetaverse setup below): `dotnet build vision/scene-mesher/SceneMesher.csproj -c Release -o <dir> -p:LMV="$PWD/textclient/src-libremetaverse"` (the same command CI uses, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`). The mesher runs with a 3 GB .NET heap cap (`GT_MESHER_HEAP`, default `0xC0000000`). |
| **A Python venv with `imagecodecs`, `pillow` and `numpy`** | `look.py` needs them (JPEG 2000 bake decode, PNG/JPG output, HDR post-processing). The text client starts `look.py` with `GT_LOOK_PYTHON` (default `/home/box/tools/imgvenv/bin/python`) and the script itself with `GT_LOOK_PY` (default `/workspace/galatea-sl-repo/vision/look.py`, `Look.cs`). E.g. `python3 -m venv /home/box/tools/imgvenv && /home/box/tools/imgvenv/bin/pip install imagecodecs pillow numpy`. Optional: `oxipng` (on `PATH` or `/home/box/tools/bin/oxipng`) shrinks the PNG losslessly; without it the PNG is kept as written. |
| **The built text client** | The export comes from a logged-in client: `bash scripts/ci-setup-libremetaverse.sh` (once), then `bash textclient/build-all.sh` (see [TESTING.md](TESTING.md)). |

**Modes**
- **Quick `look`** (`look [self|around|at <name>] [fast] [far]`, her own vision): near scene (32 m export, `self` 8 m), the fixed neutral sun/sky (the client exports with `noenv`, so the parcel or region environment is ignored), small and fast. JPG in `GT_LOOK_OUT` (default `/workspace/secondlife/vision/look`). Since 2026-10-14 quick scene renders default to exposure -1.5 stops (`QUICK_EXPOSURE` in `vision/cloud-render/render_mesh.py`); an explicit `GT_EXPOSURE` wins.
- **`photo hd [self|view|<avatar or object>] [landscape|portrait] [<sky preset>]`**: a 4K still (3840x2160, portrait 2160x3840; `self` defaults to portrait) in the real surroundings with terrain, water and sky. It follows the shared environment (parcel EEP, else the region's) unless a preset is named; the default render profile is `ultra` (David's Firestorm Ultra; `highplus` is the other). Output in `GT_PHOTO_OUT` (default `/workspace/secondlife/photos`): a Firestorm-tonemapped **PNG** (16-bit RGB, about 30 MB at 4K; `oxipng` only trims it a few percent), a quality-95 **JPG**, and the scene-linear HDR masters (**`.exr`** and **`.hdr`**) with the same stem. It takes minutes, so the command returns early and the paths are logged as `[look]`. Depth of field is on only when the photo has an avatar subject (`self`, or a named avatar). Details: [docs/photo-hd-environment.md](docs/photo-hd-environment.md), [docs/firestorm-graphics-audit.md](docs/firestorm-graphics-audit.md). Without the client, run it on an export directory: `look.py <export dir> {view|self|around|at <name>} [--fast] [--far] [--hd[=landscape|portrait]] [--sky=<preset>] [--profile=ultra|highplus] [--fov=<deg>] [--no=ssr,mirrors,dof,smaa,sharpen,aces,impostors]`.

### Texture cache
All renders fetch textures through `vision/cloud-render/handler.py` (`fetch_tex` / `fetch_textures`), modelled on the viewer's texture cache: the **raw JPEG2000 bytes** are kept once per UUID in `<work>/tex-raw/<first hex digit>/<uuid>.j2c`, and each size is **decoded from them** into `<work>/tex/<uuid>.<size>.png` (lossless, PNG level 1). A different size or discard bias (it follows free memory) therefore costs a local decode, not a download. Both tiers are capped (2 GB each by default) and evict the oldest-used files down to 80% when over; a hit refreshes a file's age. Decoding runs on a process pool (PIL holds the GIL). A texture the CDN never answers is remembered for 6 h in `tex/.missing.json`, so it no longer costs a 20 s timeout per photo. The old uncapped `<uuid>.png` files are deleted once per work dir (marker `.legacy-retired`); bakes and `.missing.json` are never touched. Measured on the Peronaut scene (3,254 textures): texture stage 157 s cold / 21 s warm / 79 s after a bias change before, about 78 / 0.5 / 11 s now. Test: `vision/tests/test_texture_cache.py`.

**Memory** (4K HD, measured 2026-10-14 on the Peronaut home scene, ~1.0 M triangles, Ultra): Blender alone costs about 3.2 GB (`HD_OVERHEAD_MB`) on top of the scene and its textures; the peak was 6.5 GB with one denoise band and 5.8 GB with three (`look.py` picks 1-3 `GT_HD_BANDS` and a texture budget `GT_TEX_BUDGET_MB` from the free memory). A 96 m export peaked at about 7 GB, so plan on roughly 7-8 GB of free memory for the render itself, on top of the guard floor below; the client chooses the export radius from free memory (256 m with 14 GB free, 128 m with 10 GB, else 96 m). `GT_MEM_FLOOR_MB` (default 1500) is a memory guard: if the box's `MemAvailable` drops under it the render is SIGKILLed so the box (and the client's UDP reads) stay responsive. A signal death (`-9`) is retried up to twice with a higher texture discard bias, a smaller texture budget, `GT_TEX_LIMIT=1024` and smaller tiles (`GT_TILE` 512, then 256); a plain crash is rerun once. One look runs at a time.

**Env vars** (all read in `vision/look.py`, `vision/cloud-render/render_mesh.py` or `Look.cs`): `BLENDER`, `SCENE_MESHER`, `DOTNET_ROOT`, `GT_LOOK_PY`, `GT_LOOK_PYTHON`, `GT_LOOK_OUT`, `GT_LOOK_WORK` (work dir; `tex/` holds the decoded texture cache and `tex-raw/` the raw JPEG2000 cache, see [Texture cache](#texture-cache)), `GT_PHOTO_OUT`, `GT_HD` (set to `1` by `look.py` for HD), `GT_HD_SAMPLES` (default 24), `GT_HD_FOV` (default 60, vertical degrees), `GT_EXPOSURE` (stops), `GT_MEM_FLOOR_MB`, `GT_MESHER_HEAP`, `GT_TEX_BUDGET_MB`, `GT_TEX_BIAS`, `GT_MAX_TEXTURES` (4000), `GT_TEX_WORKERS` (24, download threads), `GT_TEX_PROCS` (decode processes, default min(8, CPUs); 1 = threads), `GT_TEX_RAW_CAP_MB` / `GT_TEX_DEC_CAP_MB` (2048 each), `GT_TEX_RAW_DIR`, `GT_TEX_MISS_TTL_H` (6), `GT_PNG_OPT` (oxipng level, default 2; `max` = slowest), `GT_PNG_ZOPFLI=1` (slower, smaller PNG). See [docs/capabilities.md](docs/capabilities.md#photos-and-renders) for the commands.

## Host setup

Run once per host (idempotent): `sudo scripts/setup-host.sh`. It installs `/etc/sysctl.d/90-galatay-udp.conf`
(`net.core.rmem_max = 16 MB`) and applies it, so the client's 8 MB UDP receive buffer is actually granted
(without it Linux silently caps the buffer and nearby chat can be lost when the box stalls; see `UdpDrops.cs`).
The client logs a `[net] WARNING: UDP receive buffer capped` line at startup when the limit is too low.

## Not included (on purpose)
No passwords, webhook URL/key, login state, chat logs, people log or notes about other residents. The client reads the password at runtime from a local secret store (`box-secrets.json`, key `card.SECONDLIFE_PASSWORD`); you need your own copy of that.
